# Publication Boundary

This portfolio includes sanitized case studies for work produced in an AI
evaluation workflow. It intentionally excludes:

- original platform prompts and instructions;
- hidden tests and scoring weights;
- private datasets and customer information;
- model trajectories and internal review records;
- local machine names, credentials, database identifiers, and account data.

The case studies focus on the engineering decisions, reliability properties,
test design, and measurable outcomes that can be discussed in an interview.
